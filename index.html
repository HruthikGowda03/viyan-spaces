About the studio

An architecture practice built on trust, detail and a love of Bengaluru light.

Since 2013 HSR Layout, Bengaluru

Viyán Spaces began with a simple conviction: a building should feel like it belongs to its place. From a modest studio in HSR Layout-2, we have grown into a full-service design and execution practice working across residential, commercial and institutional projects.

Our team of architects, interior designers and site engineers handles everything from the first site visit to the final styling visit. That single-point accountability means fewer surprises, clearer timelines and a result that matches the drawings you approved.

  • 02

  • 03

How we work

A clear path from idea to keys.

Five stages, fixed deliverables, weekly updates. You always know what is happening on your site.

  1. 1 Week 1

    Discovery & Site Study

    Consultation, site measurements, soil and sun analysis, budget alignment and a written brief.

    Deliverable · Design brief
  2. 2 Week 2–4

    Concept & 3D Views

    Concept layouts, material palette, 3D renders and walkthrough so you can walk the home before it exists.

    Deliverable · Presentation set
  3. 3 Week 5–7

    Detailed Drawings

    Working drawings, structural and MEP coordination, BOQ and a locked specification with cost sign-off.

    Deliverable · GFC drawings & BOQ
  4. 4 Week 8+

    Execution & Site Updates

    Execution by our own team with weekly photo reports, quality checks and milestone-linked payments.

    Deliverable · Weekly progress report
  5. 5 Handover

    Handover & Warranty

    Snag-free handover, documentation, styling support and a one-year workmanship warranty.

    Deliverable · As-built & warranty
